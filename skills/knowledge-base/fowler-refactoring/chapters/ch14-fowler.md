# Chapter 14: Refactoring Tools

## Core Idea
This chapter (contributed by Don Roberts and John Brant) discusses the role of automated refactoring tools.

## Key Concepts
- **Automated Refactoring**: IDEs can perform many refactorings (Extract Method, Rename, etc.) automatically and safely by analyzing the abstract syntax tree.

## Key Takeaways
1. Use automated tools when available; they are faster and less error-prone than manual refactoring.
2. Even with tools, you still need to understand the mechanics and principles of refactoring.\n
