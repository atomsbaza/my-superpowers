# Appendix: Self-Assessment Questions

Use these questions to test whether the architecture concepts have become decision skills rather than vocabulary.

## Foundations

- What are the four dimensions that define software architecture?
- What is the difference between an architecture decision and a design principle?
- What are the First and Second Laws of Software Architecture?
- Which technical breadth/depth balance does the current role require?

## Modularity and Characteristics

- What kind of connascence exists between the two most coupled modules?
- Is that connascence static or dynamic, local or cross-boundary?
- Which characteristics influence the structure, matter to success, and can be measured?
- Which qualities are explicit, and which are implicit in the domain?

## Scope and Components

- What is the architecture quantum of the system?
- Which components are independently deployable and functionally cohesive?
- Are boundaries domain-partitioned or technically partitioned, and why?
- Did the team fall into the entity trap?

## Architecture Styles

- Which style best fits the prioritized characteristics and team capabilities?
- Which of the eight distributed-computing fallacies does each network boundary risk?
- Where are data ownership, ordering, consistency, and compensation defined?
- What would make a modular monolith preferable to microservices today?

## Decisions and Risk

- Which choices are architecturally significant?
- Can the team explain the context and consequences of each major decision?
- What are the highest impact/likelihood risks in the current architecture?
- Has risk storming included developers and operators, not only architects?

## Communication and Leadership

- Can the architecture be explained at context, container, and component levels?
- Are diagrams serving the audience or protecting an artifact?
- Does each architecture rule include a reason and a way to verify it?
- Is the architect being pragmatic yet visionary, with communication, collaboration, clarity, and conciseness?
