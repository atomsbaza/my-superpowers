# Chapter 11: Design it Twice

## Core Idea
Designing software is hard. Your first design is rarely the best one. Designing a module twice (or exploring multiple alternatives) produces significantly better results.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Design it Twice**:
  - When to use: For any non-trivial module, class, or architecture.
  - How: Do not just write the first design that comes to mind. Sketch out at least two radically different approaches. Compare their interfaces, their depth, and their information leakage. Then pick the best one (or combine them).

## Key Takeaways
1. The human brain tends to latch onto the first workable idea. Force yourself to find a second one to break that anchor.
2. "Design it twice" is an investment that pays off by preventing deep structural mistakes.\n
